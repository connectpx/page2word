P2W Page2Word Extension settings

DOCUMENT FORMAT

Formatting settings

Set the defaults used in the editor and in new Word exports. Font-size numbers are points in Word and matching pixels in the editor. Choose whether original website links are kept or removed.

Titles are centered and bold; headings are bold. Tables use a borderless layout with rules above and below the header. Figure captions retain their original label, use body text size, are left-aligned, and have a separator. Figure and table references navigate within the article.

Settings are stored locally in Chrome.
