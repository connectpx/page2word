Formatting settings

Set the defaults used in the editor and in new Word exports. Font-size numbers are points in Word and matching pixels in the editor. Choose whether original website links are kept or removed.

Titles are centered and bold; headings are bold. Tables use a layout with rules above and below the header and at the bottom. Table text is single-spaced by default. Figure captions use the “Figure” label, 11 px, are left-aligned, and have a separator. Table caption labels are bold and not linked. Figure and table references navigate within the article.

Settings are stored locally in Chrome.
